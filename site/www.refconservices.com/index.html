About REFCON

Reliable field service for commercial, industrial, and marine environments.

REFCON Services provides competitively priced commercial, industrial, and marine HVAC, refrigeration, heating, controls, construction, logistics, and engineering support for customers with immediate facility and shipboard needs.

The company brings 78 years of combined facility and shipboard repair and maintenance experience. REFCON is one of the few A/C and refrigeration companies in the Southeastern Virginia region with the ability to maintain and repair both commercial and marine equipment.

REFCON stands for responsive service, customer-focused coordination, efficient schedule support, and superior quality craftsmanship at every opportunity.

REFCON Services is an ISO 9001:2000 Certified Company with procurement statuses that support competitive bid opportunities, including Small, Women-owned, and Minority-owned Business (SWAM), Women-Owned Small Disadvantaged Business (WOSDB), and Virginia Small Business and Supplier Diversity (VSBSD) Micro Business certification.
